PerfAgent finds, explains and fixes web and AI performance problems — down to the exact file and line.

A local dashboard for your project: a globe of your code with glowing problems, a deep dive with the root cause chain and the fix, plain-language charts, and your own AI to fix it on a branch you review. 

npm i -D perfagent && npx perfagent init && npx perfagent

Local only

Runs on 127.0.0.1 with a secret token. Nothing is uploaded. No telemetry.

Read-only by default

Code changes only in local mode, on a new branch, after your review. It never pushes or deploys.

Your AI, your tokens

Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini or a company endpoint with your key in your keychain, or free local Ollama. No keys of ours.

Quick test any URL

Runs Google PageSpeed Insights from your browser (mobile). No key, nothing stored. For file-and-line causes, run npx perfagent in your project.

3D globe

Folders as rings, files as points, problems glowing by severity and gain. Click to fly to the cause.

Chips

Filter by metric (LCP, INP, CLS, TTFB, TBT, AI first token, AI cost) or by area, consistently across every screen.

Deep dive

file:line, root cause chain, why, before/after fix, estimated gain, re-test, open in your editor.

Flow diagram

Root to any depth, left to right, problem counts on every branch.

Charts in plain language

Eight tabs; every chart is a question with a one-sentence answer. "In simple words" needs no AI.

Compare anything

Before/after, page vs page, release vs release; scans, URLs, Lighthouse or PageSpeed JSON, HAR, traces, CSV, PDF, Excel, Word.

A guide for every feature

31 guides with written steps and animated walkthroughs; English, Hindi, Marathi.

Live mode

Requests, latency, WebSocket reconnects and memory every second.

AI performance

watchAI() records first token, tokens, cost, cache hits and errors; 15 static AI rules find problems in code.

MCP

Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, Copilot, Cline, Zed: npx perfagent mcp.

Fix loop

Propose, review, apply on a branch, test, re-test, undo.

Budgets in CI

npx perfagent ci fails the build and writes a pull-request comment.
